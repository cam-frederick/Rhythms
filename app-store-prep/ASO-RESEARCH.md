Most habit apps assume you do the same things every day. Life is messier than that. Rhythms gives you 8 different schedule types so you can track habits the way they actually happen:

• Every day — classic daily habits
• Weekdays only — work routines
• Weekends — leisure and recovery habits

• Every N days — "every 3 days" for training cycles
• Flexible weekly — "at least 3 times this week"
• Flexible monthly — "at least 10 times this month"

No other habit tracker offers this much flexibility. Because real routines don't fit neatly into checkboxes.
